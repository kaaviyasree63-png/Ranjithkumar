About me

Designing With Purpose.

I'm Ranjith Kumar V, a UI/UX Designer passionate about creating intuitive, accessible, and visually engaging digital experiences.

My work combines user-centered design principles, structured problem-solving, and a strong eye for visual detail to transform ideas into meaningful interfaces.

With a background in creative design and client-focused projects, I have developed skills in wireframing, interactive prototyping, responsive UI design, and reusable design systems.

I'm also exploring AI-powered design workflows to accelerate prototyping and improve design efficiency.

My goal is to create digital products that balance user needs, business objectives, and visual clarity.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Good design is not just about how an interface looks. It is about how clearly it communicates, how naturally it works, and how effectively it serves its users.

My process

From brief to handoff.

  1. Discover & Understand

    Understand business goals, user needs, and project requirements.

  2. Plan & Structure

    Organize information architecture, user flows, and screen structure.

  3. Wireframe & Design

    Explore layouts and develop polished interfaces.

  4. Prototype & Validate

    Create interactive prototypes and review usability and consistency.

  5. Refine & Handoff

    Incorporate feedback and prepare organized design files for handoff.

A proposed workflow for new projects, not a claim about every past project.
